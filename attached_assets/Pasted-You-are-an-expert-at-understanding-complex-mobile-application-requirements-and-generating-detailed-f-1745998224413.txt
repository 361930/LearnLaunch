**Context:**

The application is named "GlobalEduConnect," a global public education platform designed to connect students and teachers worldwide through a dedicated mobile application. This application must run seamlessly on both Android and iOS devices. It features role-based access, class scheduling, reporting, and donation systems. **Application Overview:**

GlobalEduConnect is a mobile-first platform that enables students to join live public classes and teachers to host them, all within a native mobile app experience. It facilitates a global educational community with features for user management, live class scheduling, content reporting, and a donation system. The application must be designed for optimal performance and user experience on both Android and iOS.

**User Roles:**

1.  **Students:**
    *   Can sign up/log in using email or Google accounts.
    *   Must provide at least their name, enrolled subjects, and interests in their profile.
    *   Can manage their profile's privacy settings (public, private, semi-public).
    *   Can view a shared schedule/calendar of live public classes.
    *   Can join live public classes directly through the app.
    *   Can donate to teachers ("Buy Me a Coffee" functionality) or directly to the admin.
    *   Can report violations (abuse, bad behavior, etc.) within the app.

2.  **Teachers:**
    *   Must register using a detailed form that includes their field of expertise, level of knowledge, and an optional teaching demo (video or file).
    *   Can manage their profile visibility settings (public or visible only to admin).
    *   Can host live classes that are open to students worldwide.
    *   Must adhere to community guidelines and maintain good ratings.
    *   Can make their profile anonymous/private if community guidelines are met.
    *   Have a "Buy Me a Coffee" donation button on their profile, accessible within the app.

3.  **Admin:**
    *   Can view, approve, or reject teacher applications.
    *   Has access to class logs (session times, teachers, attendees) within the app's admin panel.
    *   Can monitor reports and overall user activity.
    *   Can receive donations from users.
    *   Moderates flagged accounts and ensures community safety.
